# Business Model Canvas: Elderly Home Safety Hub (Singapore Context)

## 1. Key Propositions (Value Propositions)
*   **One-Stop Ageing-in-Place Ecosystem:** Integrating professional home hazard audits, physical barrier-free modifications, and ambient smart monitoring into a unified, stress-free customer experience.
*   **Frictionless Subsidy Interfacing:** Handling end-to-end administration for local grants (e.g., HDB EASE, Senior's Mobility and Enabling Fund, CareShield Life) so families don't face complex government paperwork.
*   **Culturally Sensitive Care Delivery:** Utilizing multilingual, locally certified assessors who understand multi-generational HDB layouts, language dialects, and the emotional nuances of elderly independence.

## 2. Customer Segments
*   **Sandwich Generation Caregivers:** Dual-income professionals aged 35–55 managing high-stress careers while funding and coordinating safety measures for aging parents.
*   **Independent Solo-Living Seniors:** Seniors residing alone in HDB studio apartments or private estates who proactively seek automated safety nets to maintain autonomy.
*   **Institutional B2B Partners:** Private nursing operators, Active Ageing Centres (AACs), community care organizations, and elder-care insurers outsourcing their home-risk screening protocols.

## 3. Channels
*   **Grassroots Community Outreach:** Physical roadshows, educational workshops, and mobile assessment clinics stationed at HDB void decks and local Community Clubs (CCs).
*   **Clinical Discharge Referrals:** Formalized integration pipelines with public hospitals, polyclinics, and rehabilitative step-down facilities preparing elderly patients for home discharge.
*   **Digital Discovery Platform:** A highly accessible web and mobile application featuring self-administered home safety quizzes, virtual walkthroughs, and direct booking engines.

## 4. Customer Relationships
*   **High-Touch Empathetic Consultation:** Co-creating structural safety plans through collaborative in-home visits alongside the senior and their extended family.
*   **Subscription-Based Peace of Mind:** Delivering ongoing tech support, hardware recalibrations, and real-time fall-alert routing to establish a multi-year, trusted partnership.
*   **Complimentary Post-Installation Audits:** Standard 6-month check-in visits to ensure the physical modifications remain sturdy and the family is using the systems effectively.

## 5. Revenue Streams
*   **Fixed Consultation & Audit Fees:** Flat-rate fees charged for professional, multi-point occupational therapy and architectural risk assessments.
*   **Hardware Sourcing & Installation Margins:** Direct commercial markups on certified anti-slip treatments, custom ramps, grab bars, and ergonomic safety furniture.
*   **Recurring SaaS Subscriptions:** Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) collected for 24/7 radar fall-detection monitoring, emergency button linkages, and predictive anomaly tracking.
*   **B2B Service Level Agreements (SLAs):** Tiered corporate retainer fees for conducting volume-based safety verifications on behalf of real estate developers or insurance providers.

## 6. Key Activities
*   **Comprehensive Home Risk Mapping:** Deploying trained site specialists to identify immediate hazards like high floor thresholds, dim corridors, or smooth shower tiles.
*   **Certified Hardware Procurement:** Sourcing and stress-testing durable, medical-grade hardware complying with local building and safety standards.
*   **Government Grant Interfacing:** Continuous compliance alignment with the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) to ensure swift subsidy execution and payouts.

## 7. Key Resources
*   **Specialized Domain Experts:** In-house occupational therapists, elder-centric interior planners, and technical hardware installation teams.
*   **Proprietary Assessment Software:** Mobile diagnostic applications utilized by surveyors to quickly auto-generate structural safety layouts and cost estimates on-site.
*   **Centralized Logistics Infrastructure:** Dedicated local warehousing storing immediate buffer inventory of smart telemetry devices, non-slip coatings, and modular access structures.

## 8. Key Partners
*   **Statutory & Regulatory Bodies:** Close working ties with the Housing & Development Board (HDB), Agency for Integrated Care (AIC), and Ministry of Health (MOH).
*   **Social Service Agencies (SSAs):** Partnerships with established local non-profits (e.g., Singapore Red Cross, Lions Befrienders) to seamlessly reach vulnerable or lower-income senior segments.
*   **OEM Hardware & Smart Tech Providers:** Strategic relationships with manufacturers of radar technology, automated ambient lighting systems, and specialized industrial handrails.

## 9. Cost Structure
*   **Human Capital & Specialist Overhead:** Monthly payroll expenses for certified occupational therapists, client managers, and skilled field technicians.
*   **Inventory & Physical Asset Acquisition:** Capital investments tied up in maintaining certified safety hardware and precision calibration instrumentation.
*   **Marketing & Community Engagement:** Operational expenditures for executing hyper-local grassroots campaigns, digital advertising, and designing bilingual print materials.

## 10. Investor-Grade Monetization Strategy
The business transitions from a traditional contractor model into a high-margin, scalable **Hardware-enabled Service (HeaS)** platform. Monetization is split into immediate cash-flow drivers and high-valuation recurring revenue.

### Core Pricing & Packaging Tiers
*   **Tier 1: SafeHome Basic (Transaction-Led)**
    *   *Target:* Mass-market HDB multi-generational families.
    *   *Monetization:* Fixed fee of S$150 for the home audit + 35% gross margin markup on physical modifications. Fully or heavily offset by HDB EASE/AIC subsidies.
*   **Tier 2: SafeHome Smart (Hybrid Subscription)**
    *   *Target:* Mid-to-high income families demanding active protection.
    *   *Monetization:* Upfront hardware installation fee (S$600 for fall-detection radar and smart panic nodes) + **S$39/month recurring subscription**.
*   **Tier 3: SafeHome Premium (Managed Care)**
    *   *Target:* High-net-worth individuals and families with solo-living seniors with mild cognitive decline or high fall risks.
    *   *Monetization:* **S$149/month premium subscription** including Tier 2 tech, 24/7 concierge triage routing, and bi-annual structural safety tune-ups.

### B2B Institutional Revenue
*   **AAC-as-a-Service (Active Ageing Centres):** Tiered SaaS dashboard licenses (S$500–S$2,000/month per centre).
*   **Developer & Insurance APIs:** Retainer fees from private developers to pre-fit active-ageing luxury condos.

## 11. Market Sizing & Financial Traction (Singapore Focus)
### Total Addressable Market (TAM) Valuation
*   **TAM:** S$400M+. Driven by Singapore's "Super-Aged" status (1 in 4 citizens aged 65+ by 2030).
*   **SAM:** S$120M. Middle-to-upper income HDB 4/5-room flats, executive apartments, and private condominiums.
*   **SOM:** S$18M. 15% of the SAM within the first 36 months.

### Unit Economics Projections
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** S$120
*   **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** S$1,650
*   **LTV-to-CAC Ratio:** **13.75x**
*   **Gross Margins:** Physical modifications: 35% – 45%, SaaS & Monitoring platform: 78% – 83%

## 12. Strategic Moats & Defensibility (The Investor "Why Now?")
*   **Government Integration Moat:** Approved vendor for AIC and HDB EASE frameworks.
*   **The Data & AI Network Effect:** Proprietary fall-detection models improve with every home deployed.
*   **High Switching Costs:** Calibrated physical hardware and family app onboarding keep churn below 2% annually.
