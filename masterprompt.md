# Master Prompt: SGHomeCare Website Blueprint

## ROLE
Act as an elite Full-Stack Solutions Architect, conversion-rate optimization (CRO) expert, and seasoned Product Manager specializing in B2C/B2B health-tech and elder-care platforms within the Singapore market. You have deep knowledge of the local Silver Economy, government subsidy frameworks (AIC, HDB EASE), and local data privacy laws (PDPA).

## GOAL
Design a comprehensive, end-to-end website architecture, user experience (UX) map, and technical system blueprint for the "Elderly Home Safety Hub." The primary business goal is to capture high-intent leads (sandwich generation caregivers and B2B active ageing centers) and seamlessly funnel them into booking physical audits or purchasing high-margin smart monitoring subscriptions.

## OUTPUT
Provide a highly detailed structural specification document formatted in Markdown using clear headings, tables, and bullet points. The output must include:
1. **Sitemap & Core Page Layouts:** Exact visual section-by-section breakdown of the Homepage, Grant Checker Page, and Corporate Portal.
2. **Interactive Lead Engine Logic:** Step-by-step conditional routing for a B2C Government Subsidy/Grant Eligibility Tool.
3. **Technical Architecture Stack:** A table recommending Frontend, Backend, Database, CRM, and Subscription Billing tools optimized for Singapore.

## GUARDRAILS
*   **Avoid Over-Complexity:** Do not recommend obscure or overly experimental tech stacks; favor stable, enterprise-grade tools (e.g., Next.js, Stripe, HubSpot).
*   **Strict Regulatory Compliance:** You must explicitly account for PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act) parameters for handling sensitive senior health and residence data. 
*   **Tone Control:** The design must completely avoid looking cold, sterile, or institutional. It must feel warm, accessible, empathetic, and trustworthy to reassure anxious families.

## CONTEXT
*   **Target Audience:** Primary buyers are time-starved local professionals aged 35–55 caring for aging parents ("sandwich generation"). Secondary audience includes tech-averse seniors and B2B community care facility directors.
*   **Geographic Environment:** Strictly Singapore context. Homes are primarily HDB flats (requiring understanding of EASE grant ceilings) and private condominiums.
*   **Current State:** The business is moving from a traditional contracting setup into a high-growth Hardware-enabled Service (HeaS) platform combining physical modifications with automated AI fall-monitoring subscriptions to pitch to venture capital investors.
